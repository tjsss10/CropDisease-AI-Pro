How the AI works

Real computer vision, explained.

The Java vision engine separates the leaf from the background, classifies every pixel (healthy green, chlorosis, necrosis, dark lesions, powdery growth, rust), counts and measures lesions, then compares the pattern with the signature of every disease for that crop.

It is an assistant, not a lab test. Low-confidence results are clearly marked so you can confirm with a second photo or your local agriculture officer.
